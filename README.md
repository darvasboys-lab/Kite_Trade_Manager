# Kite Trade Manager V4.1 — 2% BUY limit buffer & 0.5%-below-strike final trigger (Windows)

## What this prototype does

You place **the first NSE CNC share buy and one short NSE stock call manually in Kite**. This PC program can create one-time broker GTTs to:

* BUY 100 more shares when price touches ₹1323.25, ₹1346.50, ₹1369.75 and ₹1393.00 for a ₹1400 CE (computed; last trigger at least 0.5% below strike). Each BUY GTT has a LIMIT set **2% above its own trigger**, rounded up to the exchange tick (₹1349.75, ₹1373.45, ₹1397.15, ₹1420.90 in this example).
* SELL 100 at ₹1280 (example)
* Poll the status of each exact GTT and linked exchange order. Cancel still-active BUY GTTs when the downside SELL triggers or an inconsistency is found.
* Display indicative combined stock + sold-call P&L; if it exceeds 50% of conditional projected full-lot gross payoff, display **CONSIDER BOOKING PROFIT**. No trade is sent from this advisory.
* Require the manually received total call premium to calculate the advisory threshold. For the illustration, ₹10,000 premium yields ₹36,750 conditional projected P&L and ₹18,375 threshold.

No direct exchange order placement, option opening/closing, P&L-triggered orders or real-time quotes. All profit booking stays fully MANUAL in Kite. The initial short call and initial stock buy stay **manual**.

## Important limitations and risks

- **NOT production tested or approved for real-money trading.** Broker calls were not tested against a live Zerodha account; mock tests are offline.
- You may hold far fewer shares than the short CE contract requires. Naked/partially covered short calls can create large losses and physical settlement/delivery issues. The app never hedges or closes your short call; actively supervise expiry, corporate actions, margin and settlement.
- The final BUY **trigger** is at least 0.5% below the call strike, but the BUY **limit** is 2% above each trigger (tick rounded up), including the final batch. At a ₹1400 call strike the final BUY GTT may fill up to ₹1420.90—ABOVE the strike. A higher limit raises fill probability, NOT certainty, and reduces covered-call profitability when executed above the trigger. All fills and uncovered-call exposure require oversight. Profit projection assumes trigger-price fills, so it may overstate actual profit.
- GTT creation does not guarantee execution. A triggered LIMIT order can remain open, fill partially or be rejected (cash, circuit, CDSL authorisation, execution price, liquidity, etc.).
- All planned one-time GTTs are staged together from one **fresh manually entered Kite stock LTP**. Zerodha requires `last_price` for each creation. No new GTTs are generated by the background worker.
- A gap-up may trigger several previously staged BUY GTTs at once; some may fail to execute at their capped LIMIT prices. Maximum total requested shares are bounded, but you must monitor fills.
- An NSE GTT may trigger when the PC is OFF. If your downside SELL fires when your PC is offline, previously created BUY GTTs may remain active and buy shares on a recovery. **No free-API design with independent GTTs can completely eliminate this risk.** You must monitor Kite and manually cancel the BUY GTTs when offline or your position/option changes.
- Monitor polls every 10 seconds while active, and should be left running. Network outage, login/token expiry and PC sleep can disable cancellation. Monitor may enter REVIEW_REQUIRED rather than trading on uncertain state.
- STOP attempts to cancel tracked GTTs, but cannot reverse broker orders already triggered, and cannot cancel any unrecorded trigger after a network timeout. Manually check Kite GTT and Orders tabs.
- Selling CNC holdings may require CDSL TPIN authorisation if no DDPI/PoA. Not handled programmatically. A sell GTT may fail.
- NSE price tick size and NFO contract lot/expiry are validated against the Kite instruments API before arming; max shares must equal actual short CE units. Examples (including 500-share lot) are NOT market contract facts.
- This workflow intentionally refuses new staging within 3 days of expiry; it does NOT protect you from early broker margin, expiry settlement, or intraday risk.
- Profit/loss returned through free API may be stale. The alert is indicative only and must be manually confirmed using Kite. It may arrive late or disappear when readings update. Do NOT use this program to make automated P&L-based trades.
- The conditional projected profit assumes ALL share buys fill at their trigger prices, not at the +2% price limits, and you later have the full share quantity at the strike; it is NOT an unconditional maximum while a short call is partially uncovered. Ignore brokerage, slippage, taxes and other costs at your own risk. Theta decay is not separately isolated: option P&L reflects all premium price changes.
- Upgrading V2/V3 does not change existing settings.json; open Rules and **Save rules** once to activate AUTO_HALF_PERCENT, enter the real premium amount and review the entire ladder. The old manual ladder cannot be staged after the update.
- The program is intentionally **one strategy / one stock / one manual short call** at a time. Separate unmanaged stock orders/GTTs are refused at attachment.

## Windows setup

1. Install **Python 3.11 or later** with the `py` launcher.
2. Extract the zip into a folder. Run `start.bat` once; it creates `.venv`, installs libraries and launches a monitor window and local Streamlit WebUI. Then open `http://127.0.0.1:8501`.
3. Test Offline Demo first. No Kite credentials required.
4. On `https://developers.kite.trade` create your own Personal API app. In the app folder, edit `.env` using Notepad and insert `KITE_API_KEY` and `KITE_API_SECRET`. Leave `ENABLE_LIVE_GTTS=NO` until you have reviewed the code and tested against Kite.
5. In **Kite Login**, use the official Kite sign-in. Paste the full redirected URL or only `request_token` and press Connect. Kite access tokens typically need renewal each trading day.
6. Open the **first BUY of shares plus the short CE manually in Kite**. Configure exact underlying symbol, exact short CE trading symbol, expiry, strike, quantities, tick size and ladder. Save.
7. Use **Kite Login > Read positions** and inspect holdings. Then live Rules tab preview, which verifies exact share quantity, short CE, contract lot/expiry, existing orders/GTTs.
8. To stage real triggers (NOT recommended until broker pilot reviewed): in your local `.env` deliberately change `ENABLE_LIVE_GTTS=YES_I_ACCEPT_RISK`, restart `start.bat`, choose LIVE mode, manually read **current** Kite stock price, preview, acknowledge risk, type `CREATE LIVE GTTS`, press button within 60 seconds while market is open.
9. Inspect every new GTT in Kite; keep PC awake and monitor window running. If leaving PC unattended or option changes, use STOP & CANCEL TRACKED GTTS and manually verify all broker orders.
10. Before a NEW trade, stop/cancel, inspect all GTTs and exchange orders, then reset tracking in Dashboard with confirmation.

**Privacy:** `.env` and `session.json` contain secrets; keep the PC private, never upload those files or share screenshots exposing them. The dashboard binds `127.0.0.1` only (PC-local).

## GitHub update workflow (Windows CMD)

This repository contains code only. Your local .env, session.json, settings.json, runtime.json and events.log are private and ignored by Git.

For the first install, keep your old non-Git installation untouched and clone a new directory:

```bat
cd /d "%USERPROFILE%\Desktop"
git clone https://github.com/darvasboys-lab/Kite_Trade_Manager.git Kite_Trade_Manager_Git
cd Kite_Trade_Manager_Git
start.bat
```

For later releases, close Streamlit/worker windows, verify that no live broker-side GTT remains unresolved, and run:

```bat
cd /d "%USERPROFILE%\Desktop\Kite_Trade_Manager_Git"
git pull --ff-only
start.bat
```

Or double-click `update.bat` for the local safety preflight and a fast-forward-only pull. The preflight does not query Kite; it does not prove no broker orders exist. Never run two separate Kite Trade Manager workers against the same account. Do not upload local credentials or token files to GitHub.

## BUY GTT limit execution policy (V4.1)

- Fixed BUY limit = ceil-to-tick(GTT trigger × 1.02). A GTT is prepared ahead of time and there is no live LTP feed to compute 2% above the price when it activates.
- SELL GTTs retain the configurable downside SELL limit buffer (default 0.5%).
- A BUY limit is a maximum execution price; the exchange generally fills at the best offer at or below the limit, but execution, quantity and timing are NOT guaranteed.
- Updating code will **not** modify existing Zerodha GTTs. Before deploying, stop local workers, cancel existing GTTs in Kite, verify outstanding exchange orders, then create a new preview and explicitly stage replacement GTTs. NEVER restage a GTT while its outcome is uncertain.
- Keep PAPER mode until independently verifying a broker preview and your account risk. This change does not create broker orders merely on git pull or launch.
